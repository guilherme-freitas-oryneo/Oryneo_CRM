# Proposal Tangibility Specification

## Goal

Make the proposal email and secure proposal page show what the customer is buying in concrete terms, using only catalogued services and verified product behavior.

## Requirements

- Personalize the proposal with customer first name, company and primary goal when available.
- Present the CRM plan, implementation and selected add-ons as separate, conditional delivery cards.
- Show concrete deliverables, commercial cadence and material exclusions without inventing scope, deadlines or performance promises.
- Use one server-generated, versioned presentation snapshot for email, web proposal and printable output.
- Keep all copy deterministic and reviewed; generate no prose at send time.
- Preserve the secure versioned proposal link, acceptance hash, idempotent email delivery and plain-text email alternative.
- Let the customer print or save the secure proposal as PDF.
- Keep older proposal snapshots readable.

## Verified commercial facts

- Plans: Start, Growth and Scale; monthly or annual subscription.
- Included user limits recorded by the intake catalog: 5, 10 and 20 respectively.
- Implementation is a separate one-time item.
- Landing page is a separate one-time project with secure review, approval and publication flow.
- Paid traffic has an initial three-month charge, independent quarterly renewal and separate media budget.
